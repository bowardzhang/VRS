Data sources & coverage

Where each country's new-passenger-car registration figures come from, how granular they are, and whether brand, model and powertrain detail is available. Every source below is official and free / open; the dashboard normalises them to complete calendar quarters so countries can be compared side by side.

Countries in this dashboard

Six national registers are integrated. "Native cadence" is how often the source publishes; the trend charts aggregate everything to complete quarters. "History" is how far back this dashboard holds the series.

Countries without free open data

Markets where new-car registration detail exists but is not published as a free, machine-readable dataset, so they are not (yet) in the dashboard.

Country What's published Why it's excluded
🇮🇹Italy Monthly registration counts, and brand/model breakdowns, exist — but through industry bodies (ANFIA, UNRAE) and the Ministero delle Infrastrutture e dei Trasporti / Motorizzazione. No free, bulk, machine-readable feed. Brand/model figures are sold commercially (ANFIA, UNRAE), and the Motorizzazione portal is interactive-only with no downloadable open dataset. ISTAT's "Immatricolazioni" series is business demography (new firms), not vehicle registrations.
Other European markets. Several countries not listed here — for example Sweden, Norway, Belgium, Austria, Poland, Denmark and Ireland — do publish open registration data through their national registers or statistics offices, and are candidates for future integration. Italy is the notable large market where no free machine-readable feed is currently available. If you know of an open source we've missed, corrections are welcome.

Each figure is sourced directly from the official national register or statistics office linked above and refreshed monthly via an automated pipeline. Brand names are canonicalised across registers for comparability. ← Back to the European overview
